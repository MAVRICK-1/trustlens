Guide

How it works

01

Read the URL

Lookalike brand names, odd characters, raw IP addresses, risky domain endings and tricks used to hide the real destination.

02

Verify the certificate

Is the HTTPS certificate valid, current, issued to the right name, and strong enough? Cross-checked against public Certificate Transparency logs.

03

Inspect the page

Missing security headers, hidden redirects, login forms that send passwords elsewhere, and scam wording.

04

Ask the community

Reports from other users are weighed in. Flag a bad site or vouch for a good one to help the next person.

Scores are heuristic signals, not a guarantee. Verify sensitive sites independently. Free, open source, and no account needed.
